## Current deadline policy

Expired 2026 cycles are useful as source context but are not displayed as active application dates. On 2026-07-19, three future official dates were encoded:

- Korea University Spring 2027 online application: 2026-08-03 through 2026-08-31, Asia/Seoul;
- Korea University Spring 2027 document submission: 2026-08-03 through 2026-09-07, Asia/Seoul;
- POSTECH Spring 2027 international application: 2026-08-25 through 2026-09-02, Asia/Seoul.

Users should verify closing times, submission channels, and document cutoffs in the official source before applying.

## Editorial review cadence

- Review active deadline records at least monthly and again before each opening date.
- Review admissions, program, and scholarship pages each cycle.
- Mark broken/unavailable sources and avoid silently retaining claims.
- Store a new academic-year record when rules change instead of overwriting historical context without a note.
